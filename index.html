About Atomy Co., Ltd.

Absolute Quality Absolute Price • Global Direct Selling Enterprise

1. Company Overview

Founded in 2009 by Chairman Han-Gill Park in South Korea, Atomy is a network marketing company operating in over 27 countries globally. Our consumer-oriented "Masstige" strategy offers products of absolute quality at absolute competitive prices.

2. R&D & Manufacturing Partnership

Atomy products are created in partnership with Kolmar BNH (a joint venture between the Korea Atomic Energy Research Institute - KAERI and Kolmar Korea), bringing advanced biotechnology and herbal formulation to everyday health & beauty products.

3. Atomy Pakistan Headquarters

Atomy Pakistan Co., Ltd. is legally registered in Pakistan with offices in Lahore (Main Boulevard, Gulberg III), Karachi, and Islamabad, providing local customer care, Pakistani Rupee transactions, and fast nationwide delivery.
